Information Security, Data Privacy & Cybersecurity

Protect your critical assets and ensure compliance with stringent data privacy regulations. We offer robust cybersecurity assessments, VAPT, and information security management systems.

Request a quoteWhat we do

What we do

7 ways we help

Implement and audit the controls that protect your data.

  1. 01

    Infosec Controls Monitoring and Statement of Applicability

    Continuous tracking of security controls to ensure they remain effective and relevant. We help you maintain an accurate, up-to-date Statement of Applicability (SoA).

  2. 02

    IT Security Audits

    In-depth reviews of your IT infrastructure to identify vulnerabilities and verify compliance with security policies. Ensures your network is resilient against threats.

  3. 03

    VAPT — Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing

    Proactive simulated cyberattacks and scans utilizing advanced online tools to expose system weaknesses. Helps patch vulnerabilities before malicious exploitation.

  4. 04

    DPIA — Data Privacy Impact Assessment

    Systematic evaluations to identify and minimize the data protection risks of a project. Ensures your processes comply with global privacy laws like GDPR.

  5. 05

    RoPA Asset Management

    Establishing and maintaining a comprehensive Record of Processing Activities. Keeps track of all personal data flow within your organization.

  6. 06

    Configuration Management

    Ensuring all IT systems and software are configured securely according to industry best practices. Reduces the attack surface of your network.

  7. 07

    Ticket and Incident Management

    Structured systems for recording, tracking, and resolving security events efficiently. Ensures swift response to minimize impact during a security breach.
