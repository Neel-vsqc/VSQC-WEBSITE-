Health & Safety Implementation & Audits

Foster a secure working environment with our comprehensive health and safety audits. We provide strategic safety metrics tracking and hazard mitigation frameworks to protect your workforce.

What we do

6 ways we help

Audits, inspections and risk studies that keep people safe.

  1. 01

    EHS Project Management

    End-to-end planning and execution of Environmental, Health, and Safety initiatives. We ensure your safety projects are delivered on time and within compliance mandates.

  2. 02

    Safety Audits and Inspections

    Thorough on-site evaluations to identify workplace hazards and assess compliance with safety regulations. We provide actionable recommendations for risk mitigation.

  3. 03

    Safety Training and Testing

    Equipping your workforce with essential safety knowledge and conducting practical drills. Ensures employees are prepared to handle workplace emergencies.

  4. 04

    Incident Investigation

    Root-cause analysis of workplace accidents or near-misses. We help implement preventive measures to ensure incidents are not repeated.

  5. 05

    Safety Performance Tracking

    Monitoring leading and lagging safety indicators using specialized metrics. Provides visibility into the effectiveness of your EHS programs.

  6. 06

    HIRA, HAZOP, JSA, LOPA

    Advanced risk assessment methodologies including Hazard Identification, Operability Studies, and Job Safety Analysis. Comprehensive tools to isolate and control high-level operational risks.
