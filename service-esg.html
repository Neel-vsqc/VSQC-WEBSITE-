ESG / Sustainability Implementation Consultancy & Assurance Assessments

Drive sustainable growth and meet global ESG mandates with our specialized sustainability frameworks. We help you measure, manage, and report your environmental and social impact effectively.

Request a quoteWhat we do

What we do

17 ways we help

Build your ESG roadmap, measure impact and report with confidence.

  1. 01

    ESG Gap Assessment

    Evaluating your current ESG maturity against global standards. We identify critical sustainability gaps and outline steps for improvement.

  2. 02

    Materiality Assessment

    Identifying and prioritizing the ESG issues that matter most to your business and stakeholders. This forms the foundation of a robust sustainability strategy.

  3. 03

    ESG Roadmap Development

    Crafting a strategic timeline to achieve your short- and long-term sustainability goals. We align your roadmap with global frameworks and business objectives.

  4. 04

    Policies and Procedures Development

    Drafting comprehensive ESG guidelines tailored to your operational reality. We ensure your policies reflect industry best practices and regulatory requirements.

  5. 05

    Environmental Initiatives Support

    Providing expert guidance to execute practical environmental conservation and efficiency projects. We help turn your sustainability goals into measurable actions.

  6. 06

    Carbon, Water & Energy Assessment and Management

    Quantifying your resource footprint to establish baseline metrics. We design management strategies to optimize usage, reduce costs, and lower emissions.

  7. 07

    Life Cycle Assessments

    Analyzing the environmental impacts of your products from raw material extraction to end-of-life disposal. Helps in designing eco-friendly products and improving processes.

  8. 08

    Human Rights Implementation

    Integrating fair labor practices and human rights protections into your corporate framework. Ensures ethical treatment of workers across your supply chain.

  9. 09

    Antibribery and Fair Trade Practices Implementation

    Establishing strict ethical guidelines to prevent corruption and ensure fair business dealings. Builds trust and compliance with international anti-bribery laws.

  10. 10

    Information Security and Data Privacy Implementation

    Aligning your ESG goals with robust data protection and privacy standards. Ensures your corporate governance covers critical digital risks.

  11. 11

    Business Resilience Implementation

    Building operational fortitude to withstand and quickly recover from environmental and economic shocks. Ensures long-term sustainability of the business model.

  12. 12

    Product Stewardship Implementation

    Managing the health, safety, and environmental impacts of different products throughout their lifecycle. Promotes responsible product development and disposal.

  13. 13

    Training (Awareness, Internal Auditors)

    Educating your team on ESG principles and training internal auditors to monitor sustainability performance. Builds a culture of corporate responsibility.

  14. 14

    Supplier ESG Audits

    Evaluating your supply chain vendors for adherence to environmental and social governance standards. Mitigates third-party risks and ensures ethical sourcing.

  15. 15

    ESG Reporting

    Compiling detailed, transparent sustainability reports aligned with frameworks like GRI or SASB. Communicates your ESG achievements clearly to stakeholders.

  16. 16

    ESG Ratings Support — EcoVadis, GEE ESG, SEDEX

    Guiding your organization through complex ESG rating platforms to maximize your scores. We streamline data collection and evidence submission for platforms like EcoVadis.

  17. 17

    Digital Transformation Links (GEE Portal)

    Leverage the GEE Portal for automated ESG ratings, supplier audits, and human rights assessments. A centralized digital hub for all your sustainability metrics.
